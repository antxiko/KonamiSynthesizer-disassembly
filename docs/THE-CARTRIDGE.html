The cartridge

32 KB with no mapper, in pages 1 and 2 (0x4000-0xBFFF), and the digital-to-analogue converter: the DAC is any write to 0x4000, 8 bits unsigned with rest at 0x80. The cartridge has no RAM: the sample lives in the computer's RAM, at 0x8000-0xBFFF, and the program switches that page between the RAM (0x4105) and the cartridge (0x410D) with ENASLT, depending on whether it has to play or to draw.

Start-up

0x4000: "AB" and INIT at 0x4054. INIT notes the slots of pages 1 and 2 (0x40D4) and copies to H.STKE an rst 0x30 to 0x406E: the program starts when BASIC has finished starting. Then it clears the variables, sets H.KEYI and, before letting interrupts in, fills the sample with silence.

The first interrupt routine (0x4118) does nothing but raise the DAC from 0 to 0x80, one step per interrupt, 129 times; then it hands the hook to the per-frame routine (0x4015). That way the speaker does not give the power-on thump.

The VRAM

SCREEN 2, with the registers at 0x4295: colours at 0x0000, patterns at 0x2000, names at 0x3800, sprite attributes at 0x3B00 and sprite patterns at 0x1800. Tiles 0 to 15 are flat squares of the sixteen colours, the font runs from 0x10 (the digits) and from 0x21 (the letters), and every screen brings its patterns, colours and names in RLE from page 2 of the cartridge (0x4358): a control byte below 0x80 repeats the next one, 0x80 + n copies n bytes, 0x80 brings another address and 0 ends.

The PSG

Only for the effects: three channels (0xD810, five bytes each) with note lists at 0xBEFE. The cursor makes effect 1, a finished operation 2, a screen change 5 and the intro 8 (0xBEBF).

Konami's Synthesizer was published by Konami for the MSX in 1986; its catalogue number is RC-741 and it is 32 KB with a DAC. All rights in the program remain with their holders. This is preservation, study and documentation work, and the cartridge image is not distributed.
