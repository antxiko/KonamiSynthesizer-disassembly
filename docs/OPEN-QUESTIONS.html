Open questions

What comes from the code but has not been tried, and what is not settled.

  • The four SHIFT screens. The tone copy, the list, the printer and LAYOUT come from the code; they are neither built from the ROM nor checked.
  • The printer. The dot dump of the screen and the wave table (0x5419, 0x5516) have not been tried on a printer, nor is it known which model the sequences at 0x54B6 are for.
  • The disk code. The save, load and delete routines through the BDOS and the menu's DISK option come from the code; nothing has been tried with a drive.
  • RC741 86 5 31. The thirteen bytes at 0xBF62 give the catalogue number and, by the look of it, a date, 31 May 1986. Which date it is is not known.
  • Fifteen tones with a name and one without. Tone 16 is wave 1 with no envelopes and no filter: it may be the blank tone on purpose.
  • The padding at 0x7135. Between the last PLAY routine and the waves there are 203 bytes that are not code and nobody reads.
  • The cycles on a real machine. What is measured (254 and 255 per sample, with the M1 wait state) is from openMSX; it has not been measured on a real MSX.

Konami's Synthesizer was published by Konami for the MSX in 1986; its catalogue number is RC-741 and it is 32 KB with a DAC. All rights in the program remain with their holders. This is preservation, study and documentation work, and the cartridge image is not distributed.
